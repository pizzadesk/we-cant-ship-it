Every description was built from one or two of these patterns, mixed to fit the card's personality:

Pattern A — The observer trap

[System does X]. [Player does Y]. [Unintended consequence Z]. [Neutral verdict].

→ "Crouch. Move slowly. Hold breath near guards. The guard walks into a wall and stays. Both of you have succeeded." (Stealth)

Pattern B — The spec vs. reality split

[Impressive technical claim]. [What actually ships].

→ "Gravity-affected, wind-adjusted, arc-calculated. Missed by six inches. The physics are working correctly." (Archery)

Pattern C — The player indictment

[Setup]. [Player makes obvious mistake]. [Game confirms it without mercy].

→ "You trusted them. The game told you not to in the environmental narrative. You did not read the environment." (Trust & Betrayal)

Pattern D — The designer alibi

[Feature with visible flaw]. [Flaw named plainly]. [Absolution granted anyway].

→ "The cape flows with physics-accurate weight and clips through the spine with physics-accurate precision." (Cloth Simulation)

Pattern E — The contained universe

[World is fully simulated]. [One thing breaks the simulation]. [Nobody comments].

→ "Wolves hunt deer. Deer eat grass. Grass regrows. By hour two the wolves are gone. The grass has quietly won." (Ecosystem Simulation)

Pattern F — The known issue as feature

[Bug or failure]. [Stated without alarm]. [Shipped anyway].

→ "Bans three legitimate players, misses the aimbotters, introduces two new exploits. Shipped with confidence." (Anti-Cheat)

Tone axes used across all patterns:

Axis	Low end	High end
Target	System	Player
Affect	Deadpan	Resigned
Accuracy	Precise technical	Vague institutional
Verdict	None given	Quietly absurd
The rule: never use exclamation marks, never editorialise, never explain the joke. The flatness is the voice.
